# VocaSaathi — 5-Minute SIH Demo Script

### Target Demo Timing: ~5 Minutes
### Theme: "Most career-guidance platforms talk to the learner. VocaSaathi talks to the family."

---

## Step 1: The Landing & The Family Dilemma (Minute 0:00 – 0:45)
- **Presenter:** "Respected judges, in India, a 19-year-old rarely decides their career alone. The family makes the decision. But parents are anxious about income, social respect, and future degrees."
- **Action:** Open Landing Page. Highlight the core hero message: *"Choosing a career is a family decision."*
- **Action:** Scroll to **Why Families Hesitate** (Income, Job Security, Social Perception, Career Growth, Higher Education, Safety).
- **Action:** Point out the dual-language toggle (English / मराठी) and Low-Literacy accessibility elements.

---

## Step 2: Instant 1-Click Onboarding with Demo Family (Minute 0:45 – 1:30)
- **Presenter:** "Let's look at a typical household in Pune. Rohan has passed 12th standard and is interested in Electrical & Technology. His parents are hesitant about income stability and social status compared to a regular college degree."
- **Action:** Click **Start Family Counselling**.
- **Action:** Click the prominent **"Load Demo Family"** button.
- **Result:** Automatically populates: Age 19, 12th Pass, Pune (MH), ₹2–5 Lakh income bracket, Marathi/English, Primary concern: Income & Social perception.
- **Action:** Click **"Enter Counselling Session"**.

---

## Step 3: Evidence-Backed Counselling in Action (Minute 1:30 – 3:00)
- **Presenter:** "Notice the layout. On the left is the Family Context with an initial Resistance Score of 72/100 (High). On the right is our Evidence Panel. In the center is the AI mediator."
- **Action 1 (Income Objection):** Parent asks / clicks: *"ITI ke baad achhi job milegi kya? पगार किती मिळतो?"*
  - **AI Response:** Acknowledges parent concern, provides local Pune placement data (78% placement rate, ₹15,000–₹22,000 starting median earnings, up to ₹35,000+ with 3 years experience).
  - **Evidence Card:** Appears on the right side dynamically: `Electrician` with `Illustrative Hackathon Dataset` badge.
  - **Audio Button:** Click `🔊 Listen` to hear speech synthesis.
- **Action 2 (Degree Stigma Objection):** Parent asks: *"Degree karna better nahi hai? Is vocational education respected?"*
  - **AI Response:** Respectful pathway comparison (Degree vs. Skill track), highlights NSQF Level 4 certification, apprenticeship stipends, and upward mobility.
- **Action 3 (Higher Education Objection in Marathi):** Parent asks: *"पुढे शिकता येईल का? Can they study further?"*
  - **AI Response:** Demonstrates vertical mobility: ITI → Polytechnic Diploma lateral entry → B.Voc / B.Tech degree eligibility.
  - **Pathway Explorer:** Shows the clickable step-by-step career progression (Technician → Senior Tech → Supervisor → Entrepreneur/B.Voc).
- **Result:** Resistance score updates in real-time: **72 → 41 (Moderate Resistance)**.

---

## Step 4: Multi-Trade Comparison (Minute 3:00 – 3:45)
- **Action:** Navigate to **Compare Trades** (`/compare`).
- **Presenter:** "Families often want to see alternatives. Here we compare Electrician vs. Solar PV Technician vs. CNC Operator across 10 empirical parameters + a tailored fit analysis for this family."

---

## Step 5: Human Escalation & Counsellor Portal (Minute 3:45 – 4:30)
- **Action:** Click **"Talk to a Counsellor"**.
- **Action:** Fill quick contact details and submit.
- **Result:** Ticket `CASE-MH-2026-084` created.
- **Action:** Switch to `/counsellor`.
- **Presenter:** "The human counsellor doesn't start from scratch! They see the full family context, the resistance score shift (72 → 41), the detected objections, and AI-recommended talking points."

---

## Step 6: Administrator Insights Dashboard (Minute 4:30 – 5:00)
- **Action:** Open `/admin`.
- **Presenter:** "For scheme administrators at MSDE or State Skilling Missions, VocaSaathi aggregates anonymous family sentiment into actionable policy data."
- **Highlight:**
  - Resistance reduction distribution across 1,240+ simulated sessions.
  - Top parental hesitation: Income (38%) and Social Status (24%).
  - District Heatmap: Pune, Nashik, Nagpur resistance hotspots.
  - Calculated AI Insight Cards.
- **Closing Statement:** *"VocaSaathi does not replace human counsellors. It empowers the first family conversation with evidence — and knows exactly when a human needs to step in."*
